Questions

What kind of businesses do you work with?

We work with entrepreneurs, SMEs, creators, and established corporations looking to expand or establish operations between Asian markets and Portugal/Europe.

How do you help build relationships between Asia and Europe?

We bridge language, cultural, and regulatory barriers by acting as your dedicated local liaison, negotiating on your behalf, and introducing vetted strategic partners.

Do you handle legal and tax matters directly?

We coordinate the practical process and strategy. Formal legal representation, visa filings, and tax advisory services are provided directly by our licensed partner attorneys and accountants.

Timelines vary based on scope—ranging from a few weeks for feasibility studies and partner matching to several months for full corporate setups and visa processing.
